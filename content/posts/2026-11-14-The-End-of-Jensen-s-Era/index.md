Dear Jensen,

As I write this, I realize you'll probably never see it. Still, I wanted to put my thanks into words.

I joined NVIDIA on December 15, 2025, almost a year ago, and I write with a heavy heart. You spent more than thirty years building this company and inspired so many of us along the way. You deserved a better ending. To me, NVIDIA will not be the same without you.

I have admired you and dreamed of working at NVIDIA since I stumbled upon a video where you talked about your "life's work." You told the story of a scientist who said to you, "Because of NVIDIA's work, I can do my life's work in my lifetime." Your perspective on the world made that possible.

That story stayed with me. Ever since, I have hoped that someday I, too, might make that kind of difference for others.

Joining NVIDIA was the proudest moment of my career. It was about more than the company: it was about working for a leader who is humble, approachable, and visionary. Your words have shaped how I think about my work. Doing what I love every day, in pursuit of my own life's work, makes this the best job I could ask for.

Finding a leader worth following was my career mission, and I found one in you. You will always be my north star.

Thank you for the lessons, the stories, and the example you set. They will stay with me as I continue my work at NVIDIA. I wish you and your family well.

Sincerely,
Peter Zhang
