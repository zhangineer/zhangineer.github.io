Hey Jensen, 

Well, when I am writing this, I realized that you'll probably never seen it, but i figured after everything that has happened, I should at least write down my thoughts. 

My name is Peter, and I have been employed with NVIDIA for almost 1 year (12/15/2025 joined). Today is an extremely upsetting day, and I write with lots of emotions. 

I've been a big fan and dreamed about joining NVIDIA ever since stumble upon a video where you talked about your "life's work", and the story you told of how a scientist researcher deeply appreciated your work so that they can achieve their "life's work in their life time" because of your vision, or rather your "perspective" of the world. 

That, is how I envisioned someday maybe I could impact others just like you do.

Joining NVIDIA is the peak of my career, though it wasn't about joining NVIDIA, rather, it's the gratification and proud of working for a leader who's humble, humility, approachable and also a great story teller. You are the only CEO whom I learned directly from and memorized your words. Getting up every day and enjoy doing my life's work is the best job I could ever ask for. I had no intention to leave NVIDIA until you retire. 

But what happened the week of 11/8/2026 made me realize how unreal and cruel the world can be, I wish it was just all a dream, or maybe an AI hallucinated and created a delusional world beyond my imagination. Yet, here we are, I see chaos, anger, insult, darkness, and despair. 

I don't want to judge or make assumptions of everyone else, so I only want to express how I feel - I stand with you. I think infidelity is wrong, it hurts the ones you love. However, your resignation means that I lost my north star as well. I am not a leader like you, and finding a leader worth following was my mission, but without such leader, my mission has lost its purpose, hence the search starts over again for me. 

You are irreplaceable to me, you are the soul and brain of NVIDIA, you are what made NVIDIA it is today. With you, NVIDIA will never be the same again.
